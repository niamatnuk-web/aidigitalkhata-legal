You can reach us at aidigital.khata@gmail.com or by phone or WhatsApp on +92 319 3887840. 2. Information that stays on your device The following is created by you and stored encrypted on your device. We do not receive it: ledgers, entries, invoices, stock records, diary notes and reports; photos of receipts, bills, items and deliveries, and voice notes you attach; your PIN (stored only as a protected hash, never as the PIN itself). Videos (Exchange module only). Only the Exchange module can attach videos to an entry; other modules never show a video button. A video is one that you record with the camera or choose from your phone or computer. It stays on your device in the app’s own “Videos” folder as an ordinary file. The app does not encrypt videos on the device itself (unlike your records, photos and voice notes): they are protected only by your phone’s own screen lock and by the way Android and iOS keep each app’s files apart. On Windows the Videos folder is a normal folder of the app. On Android, the app’s folder may be visible if the phone is connected to a computer or opened with a file manager. On Android and iOS the app makes a video smaller (about 720p) once, when you attach it; on Windows the original is kept. Videos are never sent to us. A video you remove goes to the app’s bin for 30 days and is then deleted for good, together with its file. 3. Security and sync. Google servers. 4. Optional backup to your Google Drive If you switch on backup, the app saves encrypted copies of your data in your own Google Drive. The backup is encrypted with a random backup key. That key is kept in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive and is bound to your Google account, so you do not need to write down or remember any words (nothing to keep or type). We do not keep the key on any server of ours, we cannot read your backup, and we cannot recover it for you. A backup file that you choose to save to your own storage or to iCloud is locked with the same key, so only your Google account (or a Trusted Gmail that you added) can open it. Videos are included in a backup only if you switch on the “Videos” option for that backup (Exchange module); they are then part of the same encrypted backup in your own Drive and never go anywhere else. Anyone who can sign in to your Google account can open your backup, so keep that account safe. If you lose access to your Google account and your device, the backup cannot be recovered, and AiDigital Khata is not responsible for that loss. 5.
