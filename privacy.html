In short: your ledgers, entries, invoices, stock, diary, documents and sheets are stored encrypted on your own device. They are not sent to us. 2. Information that stays on your device

The following is created by you and stored encrypted on your device. We do not receive it:

  • ledgers, entries, invoices, stock records, diary notes, documents, sheets and reports;
  • photos of receipts, bills, items and deliveries, and voice notes you attach (the database copy is encrypted; see the readable copies below);
  • your PIN (stored only as a protected hash, never as the PIN itself).

Videos (Exchange module only). Only the Exchange module can attach videos to an entry; other modules never show a video button. A video is one that you record with the camera or choose from your phone or computer. It stays on your device in the app’s own “Videos” folder as an ordinary file. The app does not encrypt videos on the device itself (unlike your records, photos and voice notes): they are protected only by your phone’s own screen lock and by the way Android and iOS keep each app’s files apart. On Windows the Videos folder is a normal folder of the app. On Android, the app’s folder may be visible if the phone is connected to a computer or opened with a file manager. On Android and iOS the app makes a video smaller (about 720p) once, when you attach it; on Windows the original is kept. Videos are never sent to us. A video you remove goes to the app’s bin for 30 days and is then deleted for good, together with its file.

Readable copies in the app’s Documents folder. When you attach a photo or a voice note, or save a PDF, the app also writes an ordinary, unencrypted copy into a folder called “AiDigital Khata Documents” (sub-folders PDFs, Photos, Voice Audios and Videos), so that you can find your files. On Android this is the app’s own storage folder, which other apps with storage access (on Android 10 and older) or a computer connected to the phone may be able to read; on Windows it is a normal folder. If you choose “show in my gallery”, a photo is also added to a gallery album named “AiDigital Documents”. These copies are not sent to us. Removing an attachment inside the app does not remove its readable copy from this folder; “Wipe Old Device Data” or deleting the files yourself does.

4. Optional backup to your Google Drive

If you switch on backup, the app saves encrypted copies of your data in your own Google Drive. The backup is encrypted with a random backup key. That key is kept in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive and is bound to your Google account, so you do not need to write down or remember any words (nothing to keep or type). We do not keep the key on any server of ours, we do not hold any copy of your backup or its key, and we cannot recover it for you. A backup file that you choose to save to your own storage or to iCloud is locked with the same key, so only your Google account (or a Trusted Gmail that you added) can open it. Videos are included in a backup only if you switch on the “Videos” option for that backup (Exchange module); they are then part of the same encrypted backup in your own Drive and never go anywhere else. The key is protected by your Google account, not by a secret that only you know, so anyone who can sign in to your Google account, and Google itself, could in principle open your backup; keep that account safe. A passphrase-protected backup mode also exists in the app; if you use it, the passphrase is the key and we cannot recover it. The app also keeps a small profile file (your names, gender and date of birth) in the same hidden Drive folder, encrypted with a key derived from your Google e-mail address, which is not a secret. When a Trusted Gmail is added, that account receives the same backup and a key copy sealed for its own Google Drive. If you lose access to your Google account and your device, the backup cannot be recovered, and AiDigital Khata is not responsible for that loss.

5. Permissions the app may ask for

The app asks only when you use a feature:

  • Camera – photos of receipts, bills, items and deliveries, scanning barcodes, and (Exchange module only) recording a video.
  • Photos and files – the app does not ask for access to your photo library or storage. A picture, a video (Exchange module only) or a file is chosen with your phone’s own picker; on Android 9 and older the app may ask to save a picture to the “AiDigital Documents” gallery album.
  • Microphone – voice notes, spoken entries and the sound of a video you record (Exchange module only).
  • Notifications – stock alerts and due-date reminders. Some phones stop apps in the background; the app has a page that opens your phone’s own notification and battery settings, and it asks for no special battery permission. It runs a short background task only to upload an automatic backup when you have switched it on.
  • Bluetooth – a thermal receipt printer that you pair.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock – an optional way to unlock the app.
  • Internet – sign-in, backup and the items in section 3.

Photos, voice notes and videos are not sent to us. The database copy of photos and voice notes is encrypted on your device; the readable copies, the exported PDFs and the videos are not encrypted on the device itself (see section 2). The app does not use the location, contacts or advertising ID, and the voice-entry feature uses your phone’s own speech recogniser, which may send the audio to the phone maker or to Google or Apple under their own policies.

6. Security

Your records and the database copy of photos and voice notes on your device are encrypted; the readable copies, exported PDFs and videos are the exceptions (section 2). The app is protected by your PIN, with timed locks after wrong attempts. The Free plan may block screenshots on some platforms. No system is completely secure, so please keep your PIN and your Google account private.

7. How long we keep information

Your accounting data and videos live on your device and in your own Drive, so you control how long they are kept. Attachments and videos you remove stay in the app’s bin for 30 days and are then deleted. When you delete your account, the records we hold for it are marked as deleted and removed after a 30-day waiting period (see section 8 and the deletion page). Support and recovery requests, and the write-once markers and proofs that protect the recovery process, are not removed by the app’s deletion and are deleted by us when you ask, or when the need ends, except where we must keep something to meet a legal duty or to resolve a dispute.

  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. Write to us at aidigital.khata@gmail.com.
