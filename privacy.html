Information we handle What Why Where it goes Sign-in details: with Google, your verified Gmail address and name; with Apple (where offered), a stable Apple user ID and, if you allow it, your name and e-mail (which can be a private relay address). No password is stored for Google or Apple sign-in. To create and recognise your account. Google Firebase Authentication, run by Google. Account records: technical records used for password reset by e-mail, account recovery requests and account deletion requests. They hold hashes and technical details, never your accounting data. To let you recover or delete your account. Google Firebase (Firestore). Anonymous sign-in: the app creates an anonymous Firebase identity (a random ID with no name or e-mail) so it can read public notices that we publish inside the app, such as announcements and app settings. To show announcements and app settings. Google Firebase. Optional usage information: anonymous device and usage details such as the operating system and app version. Sent only if you agree, and you can switch it off. To understand which platforms are used and to improve the app. Google Firebase (Firestore). Optional shared slip layouts: only if you switch on “Share slip layouts anonymously”, the app shares the position boxes of fields on a slip layout. It does not share the document content or any personal data. Off by default. To help the scanner recognise common layouts. Google Firebase (Firestore). Approximate location: when you tap “Detect my location”, your internet address is sent to the public service ipapi.co, which returns a country, state and city to pre-fill a form. This happens only when you tap the button. The app does not use GPS and does not ask for device-location permission. To pre-fill your business address. ipapi.co. Connection and time checks: simple internet requests (for example to Google) to see whether you are online and to check the true date and time, which protects PIN-lock timers from clock changes. Security and sync. Google servers. 4. Optional backup to your Google Drive If you switch on backup, the app saves encrypted copies of your data in your own Google Drive. The backup is encrypted with a random backup key. That key is kept in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive and is bound to your Google account, so you do not need to write down or remember any words. We do not keep the key on any server of ours, we cannot read your backup, and we cannot recover it for you. Anyone who can sign in to your Google account can open your backup, so keep that account safe. If you lose access to your Google account and your device, the backup cannot be recovered, and AiDigital Khata is not responsible for that loss. 5. Permissions the app may ask for The app asks only when you use a feature: Camera – photos of receipts, bills, items and deliveries, and scanning barcodes. Photos and files – a picture or file you choose to attach or import, and saving exports. Microphone – voice notes and spoken entries. Notifications – stock alerts and due-date reminders. Bluetooth – a thermal receipt printer that you pair. Fingerprint or face unlock – an optional way to unlock the app. Internet – sign-in, backup and the items in section 3. Photos and voice notes stay encrypted on your device and are not sent to us. 6. Security Data on your device is encrypted. The app is protected by your PIN, with timed locks after wrong attempts. The Free plan may block screenshots on some platforms. No system is completely secure, so please keep your PIN and your Google account private. 7. How long we keep information Your accounting data lives on your device and in your own Drive, so you control how long it is kept. Account and recovery records on our Firebase project are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete your account (see section 8), except where we must keep something to meet a legal duty or to resolve a dispute. 8.
