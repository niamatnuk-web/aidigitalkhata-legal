Delete Your Account and Data

AiDigital Khata · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Deleting an account is permanent. It removes your account from our servers and ends your access. Your accounting data is stored on your own device and in your own Google Drive, so please read “What is deleted and what is not” below before you start.

Option 1: delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open AiDigital Khata and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > Delete Account & Data. You can also use Profile > Account Security > Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning and tap the delete button.
  4. Enter your PIN when the app asks.
  5. Confirm in the first dialog, then type the word DELETE to confirm for the second time.
  6. Optional: tick “also delete my cloud backups from my Google Drive” if you want the app to remove the backup copies it saved in your Drive.

If your phone is offline, the request is saved and carried out as soon as you are back online. When it finishes, the app signs you out.

Option 2: you cannot open the app

If you lost your device, forgot your PIN or cannot sign in, send us a deletion request:

  1. E-mail aidigital.khata@gmail.com with the subject Delete my AiDigital Khata account.
  2. Write from the e-mail address you used to sign in (your Gmail, Apple or account e-mail), so we can check that the account is yours. Do not send your PIN or password. We never need them.
  3. We will reply to confirm and carry out the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is not

WhereWhat happens
Our servers (Google Firebase)Your account record and the related records we hold (sign-in record, recovery and deletion queue entries, and any optional anonymous usage records linked to your account) are deleted. This cannot be undone.
Your Google DriveBackups are saved in your own Drive, and we cannot open or delete them for you. Tick the Drive option in Option 1 to have the app remove them, or delete them yourself in Google Drive. Your backup key is bound to your Google account; if you lose access to that account they cannot be opened by anyone, including us.
This deviceDeleting the account signs you out but does not erase the ledgers stored on your phone or computer. To remove them, use Backup & Restore > Wipe Old Device Data, or uninstall the app. Export a copy first if you may need your records, because wiped data cannot be restored without a backup.

Delete only one business, not the account

If you only want to remove one business, use Delete Business in the app. It goes to the Recycle Bin and stays there for 30 days before it is removed.

Keep a copy first

Before you delete, you may want to export your reports and a backup of your data (Backup & Restore) so that you keep your own records. Once the account and the copies are deleted they cannot be recovered.

Information we may keep

We may keep limited information if the law requires it or if it is needed to resolve a dispute or to prevent fraud. We will delete it when that need ends.
